---
name: Portfolio content accuracy
description: Keep portfolio biography, experience, projects, certifications, and links grounded in supplied sources.
---

Use details explicitly supplied by the user or verified in their resume. Do not invent dates, project URLs, metrics, certifications, or contact details. When a project link is not supplied, leave it empty and show the requested unavailable state.

**Why:** The user asked for portfolio content to match their resume exactly and explicitly said not to invent information.

**How to apply:** For future edits to the portfolio's About, skills, projects, experience, certifications, or links, use only resume-backed or user-provided details; ask for missing specifics rather than fabricating them.
